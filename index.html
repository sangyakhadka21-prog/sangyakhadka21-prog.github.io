01

Robotic Arm — Forward Kinematics & Joystick Control

ME 4640 Robotics  ·  2026

Designed, assembled, and programmed a 4-DOF robotic arm with real-time joystick control. Forward kinematics compute end-effector position from joint angles on-board the Arduino. Signal conditioning includes deadband filtering and exponential low-pass filter. Validated against physical measurements across three configurations.

Arduino CPython Forward KinematicsServo Control Signal Filtering
Hardware:4× servos, Arduino, dual joysticks, Vandal Robotics shield
Links:L1=100, L2=120, L3=100, L4=80 mm
Accuracy:FK validated to <5 mm across workspace

Verification: 3 configurations with expected vs. measured X/Y/Z end-effector positions

02

Inverted Pendulum — PD Feedback Control System

ME 313 Dynamics & Controls  ·  2025

Full closed-loop PD feedback control of an inverted pendulum using Arduino and MEMS IMU. Complementary filter fuses accelerometer and gyroscope data. Gains tuned in two stages: 50% overshoot target, then optimized for minimum settling time. Experimental results validated against MATLAB simulation.

PD ControlArduino C MATLABIMU / Sensor Fusion Signal Processing
Controller:kp = 6 deg, kd = 0.25 deg/s (optimized PD)
Result:~40% reduction in settling time vs. overshoot-tuned
Output:PWM torque ±255, constrained via Arduino

Fig A — Overshoot controller (~50%): angle and torque vs. time

Fig B — Optimized PD (kp=6, kd=0.25): faster settling, proportional/derivative torque breakdown

03

Silicon Microfabrication — Cleanroom & TSV Processing

University of Idaho Cleanroom  ·  2025–26

Fabricated 3D semiconductor devices using through-silicon via (TSV) technology in a Class 100 cleanroom. Processes included silicon wafer grinding and CMP polishing, copper electroplating, thin-film deposition, and wafer bonding. Results verified with optical microscopy. Contributed to an accepted ASME InterPACK 2026 paper.

CleanroomTSV Copper ElectroplatingSolidWorks MicroscopyWafer Bonding
Tools:CMP, electroplating bath, optical microscope, cleanroom
Publication:ASME InterPACK 2026 — Abstract Accepted

Left: SolidWorks exploded TSV wafer stack model  ·  Right: Optical microscopy cross-section showing copper via pillars

04

Rat Race — Autonomous Arcade Game

ME Mechanical Design  ·  Team 41  ·  2024

Designed and built a two-player autonomous arcade-style rat race game. RC nano-racer cars navigate a wooden obstacle track with angled deflectors. System includes a servo-actuated drop gate for simultaneous release, IR break-beam lap detection, automated scoring, and a gravity-return system. Full track modeled in SolidWorks.

SolidWorksArduino MATLABIR Sensors Mechanism Design
Features:Drop gate, return system, IR timing/scoring
Design goal:Autonomous, 30+ min play, ages 5–10

General track design: dual-lane obstacle course with start gate and shared finish chute

Key systems: RC controls, drop gate mechanism, return system, and IR timing
